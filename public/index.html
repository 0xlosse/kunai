How to play

  • Match the color or the number/symbol of the top card. First to empty their hand wins.
  • Block skips the next player. Rewind reverses order. Spray +2 makes the next player draw 2.
  • Wildstyle picks any color. Bomb +4 picks a color and makes the next player draw 4.
  • Bluff mode: play any card face-down and claim it is a legal card. The next player accepts or calls Liar! A caught liar takes the card back and draws 4. A wrong accusation costs 2.
  • Skills: every card you play adds 1 charge. At 4 your crew member's skill is ready. Use it on your turn before drawing. Skills don't end your turn.
  • Scoring: the winner gets the value of every card left in other hands. Numbers count face value, action cards 20, wild cards 50.
