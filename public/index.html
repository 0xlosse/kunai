How to play

  • Match the element or the number/symbol of the top card. First to empty their hand wins.
  • Smoke Bomb skips the next player. Mirror Scroll reverses order. Kunai +2 makes the next player draw 2.
  • Scroll picks any element. Dragon +4 picks an element and makes the next player draw 4.
  • Bluff mode: play any card face-down and claim it is a legal card. The next player accepts or calls Liar! A caught liar takes the card back and draws 4. A wrong accusation costs 2.
  • Skills: every card you play adds 1 chakra. At 4 chakra your shinobi's skill is ready. Use it on your turn before drawing. Skills don't end your turn.
  • Scoring: the winner gets the value of every card left in other hands. Numbers count face value, action cards 20, wild cards 50.
